#+title: Kernel choice and the hibernation crash

nixOS runs the 7.2 series of Linux, selected by =boot.kernelPackages= in [[file:../../hosts/nixOS.nix][the host configuration]]. This page records the
crash that decided it, the kernels that carry the defect, and when to change the choice.

* The crash

On 4, 8 and 18 September 2026, under Linux 7.2.0, 7.2.3 and 7.2.4, the laptop froze or restarted between a few minutes
and a few hours after it resumed from hibernation. Each time, the kernel log, or the pstore record when the journal had
stopped, showed a fault in TTM, the memory manager that amdgpu uses for graphics buffers:

#+begin_example
list_del corruption
ttm_lru_bulk_move_del
amdgpu_vm_pt_free
#+end_example

or:

#+begin_example
Oops: general protection fault
RIP: ttm_lru_bulk_move_tail+0x131/0x1d0 [ttm]
amdgpu_vm_move_to_lru_tail [amdgpu]
amdgpu_cs_ioctl [amdgpu]
#+end_example

Soft lockups followed in every process that drew on the screen. The application named in a trace, such as Loupe or
Nautilus, only submitted the graphics work that reached the damaged list.

* The defect

Commit [[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/b2ed01e7ad3d][b2ed01e7ad3d]], "drm/ttm: Fix ttm_bo_swapout() infinite LRU walk on swapout failure", moved the removal of a
swapped-out buffer from its bulk move range under =if (!ret)= after =ttm_tt_swapout()=. That function returns the number of
pages swapped out on success, so the removal never ran. Hibernation swaps graphics buffers out; a buffer freed later
stayed in the range, and the next walk of the range read freed memory.

Commit [[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/3db7d7d58341][3db7d7d58341]], "drm/ttm: fix swapped-out resources never leaving their bulk_move range", tests =ret > 0= instead,
and closes [[https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/drm/amd/-/issues/5387][drm/amd issue 5387]]. In mainline its first application changed the wrong condition, which [[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/fcfe64715b42][fcfe64715b42]]
corrected. The stable 7.2.8 release carries both as one change; [[https://cdn.kernel.org/pub/linux/kernel/v7.x/ChangeLog-7.2.8][its changelog]] describes the failures that the traces
above show.

The kernels that predate the defect had no such crash here: none of the 16 hibernations recorded from 4 to 20 October
2026, on 6.18 releases before 6.18.57, was followed by one.

* Kernels that carry the defect

Checked on 20 October 2026 in =drivers/gpu/drm/ttm/ttm_bo.c= of each release:

| Kernel             | Code after =ttm_tt_swapout()= | Status       |
|--------------------+-----------------------------+--------------|
| 6.18.50 to 6.18.56 | Removal before the swap-out | Not affected |
| 6.18.57            | =if (!ret)=                   | Defect       |
| 7.2.0 to 7.2.7     | =if (!ret)=                   | Defect       |
| 7.2.8 and later    | =if (ret > 0)=                | Fixed        |
| 7.3                | =if (ret > 0)=                | Fixed        |

The defect was tagged for the stable releases from 6.13, and [[https://cdn.kernel.org/pub/linux/kernel/v6.x/ChangeLog-6.18.57][6.18.57]] took it as a dependency of [[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/1d59f36e95f7][1d59f36e95f7]], "drm/ttm:
Fix ttm_bo_shrink() infinite LRU walk on backup failure". The fix is tagged for 7.1 and later only, so no 6.18 release
had it on that date.

* History of the choice

| From              | Selection            | Reason                          |
|-------------------+----------------------+---------------------------------|
| Until 22 Sep 2026 | =linuxPackages_latest= | Default; 7.2.0 to 7.2.4 crashed |
| 22 Sep 2026       | =linuxPackages_6_18=   | 6.18.50 predates the defect     |
| 20 Oct 2026       | =linuxPackages_7_2=    | 6.18.57 brought the defect back |

* Alternatives considered

On 20 October 2026, two other options had the fix:

- 7.3, through =linuxPackages_latest=, has the fix. It was two days old, and it adds new TTM code, for cgroup charging, in
  the same subsystem.
- 6.18.57 with the fix applied through =boot.kernelPatches= keeps the longterm branch. The kernel would then be built
  locally after every lock update, until a 6.18 release carries the fix.

Holding 6.18.56 was rejected: it receives no further security fixes.

* When to change it

7.2 reaches its end of life a few weeks after the release of 7.3, and nixpkgs then removes =linuxPackages_7_2=, so a lock
update makes the configuration fail to evaluate. By then 7.3 has had point releases. Move to =linuxPackages_latest=, or to
a longterm branch whose release has the fix. To check a release, here 6.18.58, read the line after the call:

#+begin_src sh
curl -s https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gregkh/linux/v6.18.58/drivers/gpu/drm/ttm/ttm_bo.c |
  grep -A1 'ret = ttm_tt_swapout'
#+end_src

=if (ret > 0) {= is the fix; =if (!ret) {= is the defect.

After any change of kernel, hibernate several times, on battery and on AC, and use the graphical session for hours after
each resume. The crash came minutes to hours later, never at once.

* Related issues with another cause

Three open Framework issues concern this platform and its sleep or freezes, but not this defect:

- [[https://github.com/FrameworkComputer/SoftwareFirmwareIssueTracker/issues/261][Framework issue 261]]: on battery, hibernation in =platform= mode returns at once instead of powering off, with no error
  logged.
- [[https://github.com/FrameworkComputer/SoftwareFirmwareIssueTracker/issues/41][Framework issue 41]]: freezes followed by a restart, recorded as a data fabric sync flood, reset reason =0x08000800=.
- [[https://github.com/FrameworkComputer/SoftwareFirmwareIssueTracker/issues/50][Framework issue 50]]: a restart on resume from s2idle, recorded as a triple fault, reset reason =0x00800800=.

The kernel prints the reset reason at the next boot:

#+begin_src sh
journalctl -b -k | grep 'reset reason'
#+end_src
