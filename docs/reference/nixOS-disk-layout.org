#+title: nixOS disk layout
#+author: piotr-yuxuan

What =disko-config.nix= creates on the NVMe drive of nixOS.

* Partitions and volumes

Disko reads =disko-config.nix= and creates:

1. A 1 GB *ESP* partition (FAT32, mounted at =/boot=).
2. A *LUKS-encrypted* partition spanning the rest of the NVMe.
3. Inside LUKS, an *LVM volume group* (=vg-nixOS=) containing:
   - =lv-swap= (72 GB): swap with resume support for hibernate.
   - =lv-btrfs= (remaining space): Btrfs with subvolumes for =/=,
     =/home/example=, =.cache=, and =/.snapshots=.

All subvolumes use =compress=zstd= and =noatime=.

* Device tree

#+begin_example
/dev/nvme0n1
├── ESP (1 GB, vfat)  → /boot
└── LUKS (cryptroot)
    └── LVM vg-nixOS
        ├── lv-swap (72 GB, swap, resume device for hibernate)
        └── lv-btrfs (rest)
            ├── @root         →  /
            ├── @example       →  /home/example
            ├── @example-cache →  /home/example/.cache
            └── @.snapshots   →  /.snapshots
#+end_example
